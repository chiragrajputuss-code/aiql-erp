## 4. About / Overview (≤2000 chars) — paste-ready

Most Indian businesses lose real money to problems they can't see in their own books — input tax credit blocked because a vendor never filed their GSTR-1, or the same bill quietly paid twice. It rarely shows up as a line item, and it's usually found months too late.

AcctQAI reads a business's purchase register and GSTR-2B and investigates them the way a sharp accountant would — surfacing:

• Invoices where ITC is at risk because the supplier hasn't filed
• ITC available in GSTR-2B but not yet booked
• Bills paid twice
• Supplier and reconciliation mismatches

Every finding comes with the supporting invoices, the rupee impact, and a clear next step — so it can be verified and acted on, not just noted.

We built AcctQAI for Indian CAs and finance teams. It is read-only — it never changes your books — and works alongside Tally and your existing ERP. Sensitive details are masked before anything is processed.

The goal is simple: help you catch the money slipping out of your books before it's gone.

Learn more at acctqai.com
