What AccountIQ Is

AccountIQ connects to a company's accounting data — Tally exports, Zoho Books, or uploaded General Ledger and GST files — and automatically investigates the books. Instead of waiting for someone to ask the right question, it proactively surfaces GST risks, vendor issues, duplicate payments, unusual expenses, cash leaks and opportunities — each with the supporting evidence, the rupee impact, and a recommended action, before month-end.

The shift: AccountIQ is moving from an "AI query tool" (you must ask) to a Financial Investigation Platform (the system investigates and tells you what to fix first). The query engine becomes a drill-down capability — not the front door.

The Core Problem

Indian SME finance teams lose real money to things they only discover after the fact:

  • Blocked ITC — a vendor doesn't file GSTR-1, so Input Tax Credit is reversed weeks later, with interest.
  • Duplicate payments — the same invoice paid twice, caught (if ever) at year-end.
  • Cash stuck in receivables nobody chased; TDS gaps; expense spikes nobody flagged.

Today this is done manually — exporting reports, rebuilding pivots, eyeballing ledgers. AccountIQ automates the investigation.

Every Finding Answers Five Questions

This is the trust model — no vague alerts, no black box. A finding isn't production-ready unless it answers all five:

#QuestionField
1What business question does this answer?businessQuestion

3How much money / risk is involved?impactRs
4What should the team do next?recommendation
5How can they verify it?verificationSteps

Eight Architecture Principles (frozen)

1Business Context is resolved before execution begins.
2Investigations declare capabilities (e.g. GENERAL_LEDGER, INPUT_TAX_CREDIT), not connectors.
3The Runner resolves dependencies — investigations never self-discover data.
4Every investigation runs against an immutable snapshot (reproducible, no mid-run drift).
5The LLM explains evidence — it never generates it. One AI-written field only.
6Evidence is materialised at run time, so a finding survives the 90-day raw-data expiry.
7Findings are superseded, never deleted — full history for trends and disputes.
8Every finding declares its resolution condition (what makes it go away).

Privacy by Design

Sensitive fields — vendor and customer names, references and amounts — are encrypted and masked before any analysis runs, and are never exposed to any AI model. The platform is strictly read-only: it analyses your books, it never modifies them. Data is stored in AWS Mumbai (ap-south-1), isolated per organisation.
